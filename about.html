About Us

Your Beauty, Our Passion

Chune Beauty Salon is a women's beauty destination located at the heart of Piazza, Hawassa. We specialise in hair braiding and styling — from box braids and knotless braids to cornrows, curls and everything in between — alongside makeup and nail services. Every client walks out feeling confident and beautiful.

We use quality products and take time to listen to every client, because your hair is unique and your style should be too. Whether you come in for a full braiding session, a quick touch-up or a special-occasion glam look, you are always welcome at Chune.
